# PURGE Benchmark Tasks

This document outlines the finalized list of the 6 datasets, their corresponding classification tasks, and the exact labels that the `ABMIL` probes will be trained to classify.

## 1. PANDA
* **Task**: Prostate Cancer ISUP Grading
* **Labels** (6 classes): `ISUP_0`, `ISUP_1`, `ISUP_2`, `ISUP_3`, `ISUP_4`, `ISUP_5`

## 2. BACH
* **Task**: Breast Histology Classification
* **Labels** (4 classes): `Benign`, `InSitu`, `Invasive`, `Normal`

## 3. BRACS
* **Task**: Breast Atypical and Malignant Lesion Subtyping
* **Labels** (7 classes): `ADH`, `DCIS`, `FEA`, `IC`, `N`, `PB`, `UDH`

## 4. UBC-OCEAN
* **Task**: Ovarian Carcinoma Subtyping
* **Labels** (5 classes): `CC`, `EC`, `HGSC`, `LGSC`, `MC`

## 5. TCGA-LUNG
* **Task**: Lung Cancer Histologic Subtyping
* **Labels** (2 classes): `LUAD` (Adenocarcinoma), `LUSC` (Squamous Cell Carcinoma)

## 6. TCGA-BRCA
* **Task**: Breast Cancer Histologic Subtyping
* **Labels** (2 classes): `IDC` (Infiltrating Ductal Carcinoma), `ILC` (Invasive Lobular Carcinoma)
