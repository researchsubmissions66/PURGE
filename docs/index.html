The Methodology

Traditional adversarial attacks perturb pixels at inference time to fool frozen models. PURGE operates at the dataset level, acting as a Targeted Latent-Space Poisoning Attack to blind downstream training.

The Scalpel vs Sledgehammer Effect

The Sledgehammer (Noise)

Applying isotropic Gaussian Noise or Feature Dropout destroys the targeted organ signature, but it indiscriminately crashes all other downstream predictive tasks to random guessing (AUC ~0.50).

The Scalpel (PURGE)

By projecting embeddings into a mathematically orthogonal null space (via SVD or CORAL Optimal Transport), PURGE surgically deletes the target concept while perfectly preserving all other clinical tasks.
